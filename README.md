# Transversal Project — Main Landing Gear

Transversal project report for the International Bachelor in Mechanical, Materials and Aerospace Engineering.

**Authors:** Fernando Martins, Gaelle Abi-Ghanem, Quang Minh, Yaroslav Knyazkov
**Supervisor:** Francois Lamoine

## Overview

This project covers the conceptualization, design, and construction of a fully mechanical scale model of an aircraft **main landing gear**, inspired by the Boeing 737's retractable gear. The model reproduces the three core systems of a real landing gear without any hydraulic, pneumatic, or electric components:

- **Load-bearing truss structure** — wheel beam, truss #1, truss #2, and the triangle section, modeled after the Boeing 737's side-stay/drag-stay arrangement.
- **Retraction system** — driven by a manually-operated, 3D-printed screw actuator (bolt-and-nut mechanism) in place of a hydraulic cylinder.
- **Self-locking mechanism** — holds the gear in place in both the extended and retracted positions without relying on the actuator.

## Report Contents

The full write-up (`Project___Transversal.pdf`) includes:

1. **Abstract & Introduction** — history and evolution of landing gear, types of landing gear (fixed/retractable, conventional/tricycle/ski), and the main components of a real landing gear assembly.
2. **Model** — design rationale, dimensioning, kinematic and free-body diagrams, locking mechanism, actuator design and testing, material selection, CAD model per part, and the manufacturing process.
3. **Conclusion** — final product summary and possible improvements (motorized actuator, stronger structural materials, refined locking mechanism, mathematical optimization of the truss and actuator).
4. **Appendix** — supporting references and figures.

## Final Product

The built model is a scaled-down, fully mechanical retractable landing gear mounted on an aluminum ELCOM frame, with PLA 3D-printed trusses/actuator and steel shafts/connectors, operated by hand via a crank-driven actuator.
